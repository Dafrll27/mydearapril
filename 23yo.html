A Quiet Gratitude

A birthday reflection

With all respect and humility,

I would like to express my gratitude to Allah SWT, who has always been with me through my most difficult and fragile moments.

Thank You for all the good things You have given me, my parents, my family, my friends, and those whose presence brings love into my life.

There is one thing I know for sure I am not perfect. There are times when I feel lost, and I know I cannot do this life on my own. And so, as a form of Your help, You placed these people in my life.

Through them, I have learned what love truly means, and what it means to sacrifice.

I am deeply grateful to be surrounded by kind-hearted people, who stay, even when I feel insecure, anxious, unsure, and like I have failed.

In those moments, You always guide me back to the right path, gently reminding me to keep moving forward, even when my heart is still full of doubt.

For all of this, I will keep learning to become a better person, to be more empathetic, and to have a bigger heart, so I can continue to love and give to those around me.

— Thank you for everything 🤍
